BRANDING DESIGNS

“Taya” is a brand of designs printed onto apparel and other physical merchandise inspired by traditional Filipino games. Taya's designs bring a modern fun with grit like motifs for a more nostalgic feel for young adults.

Hello world!

I’m Ellese, a multimedia student putting most of my energy into graphic design and film production, as I’ve always been drawn to visual storytelling whether through clean branding or a well-shot scene. Design lets me mess around with layout, color, and typography, while on the video side, I really enjoy bringing an idea all the way from a rough script to the final edit.

Combining both fields keeps things interesting and gives me a pretty broad perspective on visual arts. Most of my free time goes into experimenting with new techniques, trying out different styles, and refining my overall aesthetic. I love taking bold ideas and turning them into polished pieces, so every project here represents a little piece of that creative journey. Getting to blend design principles with cinematic visuals is definitely my sweet spot, and I treat every project as a chance to level up. Honestly, I’m just really passionate about creating stuff that catches people’s eye, and I hope you dig what I’ve put together!
